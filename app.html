Built for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. Quorum opens on a demo mission: a 14-sol sample crew history that shows every screen working. “Start fresh” clears it, and from then on Quorum learns only from your own check-ins, stored on this device. Thresholds are simplified for demonstration. The Labs screen and the Proof page use real, de-identified Inspiration4 blood data from NASA OSDR (OSD-569, OSD-575); the control-kit count on the Labs screen is a simulated overlay. Not a medical device. Anatomy figure adapted from react-muscle-highlighter (MIT); icons from Tabler Icons (MIT).
